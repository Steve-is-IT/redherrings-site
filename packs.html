Author a pack, sell it here

Pro licenses can author and resell packs. Build cases in the Scenario Editor, prove them with Verify draft, export the pack, and send it in. We list it with your name and publisher line, handle checkout and delivery, and pay out quarterly.

01

Build and verify

Write the case in the editor. Verify draft generates one variant and runs the Fresh Catch check so every answer is proven before anyone sees it.

02

Export and submit

Export the pack from the Tackle Box with your publisher line and version, then send it through the contact form under Something else with the subject Pack submission.

03

Listed and paid

We test it on a clean machine, set the price with you, and list it. Buyers get a signed download; you get a statement and a payout each quarter.
