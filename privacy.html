Privacy Policy

The short version: Red Herrings runs on your computer, and the labs you build (evidence, rosters, student answers, grades) stay on it, except that LTI connected mode, if your institution runs it, posts grades to your LMS. None of it comes to us. We collect only what we need to sell and license the software.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Red Herrings is a product of The Competence Collective, LLC ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what we collect through our website, licensing service and account pages, and how the desktop application handles your data. Questions? Use the contact form or email sales@redherrings.app.

1. The application runs locally

The Red Herrings desktop application generates and grades labs entirely on your own machine. It doesn't send us the cases you generate, the evidence files, your class rosters, student names, submitted answers, grades, or any file you import or export. None of that touches our servers at any point.

The application contacts us over the network only to activate, refresh, or deactivate a license key (see section 5), and when you ask for a trial key in the app, which sends us the email address you enter. You can also activate offline, entirely by file exchange, on an air-gapped machine.

Two other connections never reach us. If you turn on the update check (it's off until you say yes), the app asks GitHub for the latest release each time it starts and when you click Check now. A running app reuses a successful answer for a day; after a failed check, it asks again the next time its page loads. Each request carries the app version, and GitHub sees your IP address. Clicking Update now downloads the release and its checksum file from GitHub. If your institution runs LTI connected mode, the copy it hosts exchanges registration, launch and grade data with your LMS, not with us.

2. What we collect

We collect a limited set of information, only in these situations:

  • When you buy a license: your name, email address and the details of your order, as Stripe passes them to us. Payment card details are entered directly with our payment processor and are never seen or stored by us. Our licensing service keeps your name, email address, plan, and the Stripe customer and subscription IDs that go with your key.
  • When you ask for a trial key, on the download page or in the app: your email address and, from the download page, the use you picked (teaching, a security team, or study on your own) and whether your browser reports Windows or Linux. We issue a 30-day trial key under that address, email it to you, and add the address to our trial follow-up emails (see section 3).
  • When you use your account at account.redherrings.app: your email address and when you signed in. You sign in with a single-use link we email you; there's no password. Your account lists your licenses and the machines activated on them, by hostname.
  • When you ask for a student or veteran discount: for the student check, the school email address we send a six-digit code to (we store a hash of the code, not the code, and keep a record that the school address was used). For a request our staff reviews: your name and what you tell us about your school and program, or your service branch, years of service and any note. An approved discount comes as a single-use Stripe promotion code.
  • When you contact us: your name, email address, organization, topic and message. The contact form stores your message, forwards it to our sales inbox and sends you a receipt.
  • When you buy or claim a scenario pack: your email address, the pack, and the Stripe checkout it came from.
  • When the app activates a license: your license key, a machine identifier, the computer's hostname and the app version, so we can enforce the number of seats on your plan (see section 5).
  • When you visit the website: page views and button clicks counted by GoatCounter, fonts loaded from Google Fonts, and standard request logs kept by our hosting providers (see section 6).

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

3. How we use it

  • To deliver your license key and fulfill your order.
  • To provide licensing (activation, seat management, renewals) and customer support.
  • To send you transactional messages about your purchase, such as receipts, key delivery, and renewal or expiry notices.
  • To follow up on a trial. A trial key request gets the key and a guide, then two more emails: one a week in, pointing to a free incident-response case, and one three days before the trial ends, about buying a plan. Each carries an unsubscribe link. Once you unsubscribe we don't send them again, even if you ask for your key again. We don't send other marketing email unless you ask to be added.
  • To keep the service secure and to comply with our legal and tax obligations.

4. Service providers

We rely on a small number of vetted providers to run the business. They process data only as needed to provide their service to us:

  • Payments: handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us; we receive only confirmation of payment and the name and email address our licensing service keeps for your license (see section 2).
  • Licensing and account server: our licensing service and account pages (account.redherrings.app) run on Render, behind Cloudflare. Render stores the records described above. Both see the IP address of each request. Our server uses it to limit how often one address can make requests, and doesn't save it with your license, trial, account or contact records.
  • Website hosting: this site is served as static files by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard access logs. App downloads and updates come from GitHub's release pages.
  • Website analytics: GoatCounter counts page views and button clicks (see section 6).
  • Fonts: the site's pages and the account pages at account.redherrings.app load their fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Email: we use an email provider to deliver keys, sign-in links and trial follow-ups, and to reply to support requests.
  • Staff alerts: our licensing service can post short alerts to a private Discord channel our team reads: a new contact message (your name or email address, the topic and the start of the message), a trial key sent or refused (your email address and the use you picked), a discount request (your email address, name and service branch), and payment, dispute and cancellation events from Stripe (your email address).

5. License activation data

To stop one key being used on unlimited machines, activation sends our licensing server your license key, a machine identifier (a value derived from your device that lets us count and manage seats), the computer's hostname, and the app version. Refresh and deactivation send the license key and the machine identifier. We store the machine identifier, hostname and app version with your license, together with activation and refresh timestamps. You see your machines by hostname in your account, and our support staff see them in our admin console.

Activation deliberately carries no lab content and no student data: not the cases you build, not rosters, not answers, not evidence. Deactivating a seat removes that machine from your active list; its record stays with your license, marked inactive. Air-gapped machines can complete the same activation by exchanging a request and response file, with no direct connection. The request file carries your key's ID, the machine identifier, the hostname and a hash of the key.

6. Cookies & the website

This marketing site doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies. It counts visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies. The pages that load it send GoatCounter their address and title, the page you came from, your screen size, and which buttons you click, and GoatCounter sees your IP address and browser like any web request. The site's pages, and the account pages at account.redherrings.app, load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address and browser too. The cases page remembers which featured case to show next in your browser's local storage; that value never leaves your browser. Our hosting provider keeps standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP address and request time) to operate and secure the site.

When you sign in at account.redherrings.app, it sets two cookies: one keeps you signed in, and one protects your requests against forgery. Neither is used for tracking.

7. Retention

We keep order and license records for as long as your license is active and thereafter as required for tax, accounting, and legal purposes. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to help you and to improve the product. Server logs are kept for a short period by our hosting provider. If you unsubscribe from trial follow-ups, we keep your address on the list, marked unsubscribed, so it isn't emailed again.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these, email sales@redherrings.app and we'll respond within a reasonable time. We won't discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Because payments and receipts run through Stripe, some requests may need to be directed there as well.

9. Students and children

Red Herrings is sold to educators, institutions, and professionals, not to children. Because labs are generated and graded entirely on the instructor's machine, we never receive your students' information of any kind. Instructors remain the controllers of any student data they enter locally and are responsible for handling it under their own institution's policies (including FERPA, GDPR, or local law as applicable).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product and our practices evolve. We'll change the "last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide a more prominent notice.
