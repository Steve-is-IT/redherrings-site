Evidence chain of custody

One form per evidence item. Every transfer, examination and return is a row in the log, signed by the person releasing and the person receiving. Keep it with the evidence and attach it to the report.

Teaching note. In a Red Herrings lab the generated evidence manifest lists every file with its hash. Have students copy those hashes onto this form, re-hash the files they received, and record the verification as the first row of the log. A mismatch is the first finding of the report, not a reason to skip the form.
