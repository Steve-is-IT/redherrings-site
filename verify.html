Verify a certificate

Every Red Herrings certificate carries a verification code computed from the details printed on it. Type those details exactly as they appear, enter the code, and we'll confirm the certificate has not been altered — entirely in your browser, with nothing sent anywhere.

This confirms the printed fields match the code. It is an integrity check, not an accredited credential; to confirm a certificate was genuinely issued, contact the issuing instructor or institution.
