Every student works a real investigation that's theirs alone.

The portal hands each learner their own unique case: real evidence, the real tools, answered in the browser. Grading happens on your machine, so the answer key is never anywhere a student could reach it.

01

A different case for every student

Each private link resolves to that student's own seeded variant of the case, with the question order optionally shuffled. No two people investigate the same evidence, so a copied answer is simply the wrong answer.

02

The answer key never leaves your computer

Red Herrings serves the portal from your own machine and grades every answer locally — only right-or-wrong comes back. There is nothing in the browser to read, scrape or share, and you reveal the full walkthrough yourself, when you choose to.

03

Practice as they learn, or sit a timed exam

One switch at generation time. Practice checks each answer as they go, with optional hints, open until you close it. A timed exam starts a server-enforced countdown, locks answers on submit, flags late work, and keeps scores yours until you release them. The paper worksheet is always included too.

04

A refresh never loses work

Answers are saved in the student's own browser as they type, so a reload mid-exam picks up exactly where they left off. That draft never leaves their device, and no student data leaves your machine.

05

Badges, certificates & class placement

Passing a case earns achievement badges and a printable Certificate of Completion; finishing a whole certification track earns another. The class leaderboard mints 1st/2nd/3rd place certificates in a click. Every certificate carries a verification code and is generated locally — proof of completion, not an accredited credential.

For the instructor

See the whole class at a glance

As students submit, a dashboard shows how the class is doing, all on your machine: the score distribution, the questions that tripped most people up, and a flag for any item nobody got, which usually means a mis-keyed answer rather than a hard one.

Already run a course in an LMS? Connect it.

Entirely optional. Deployed as a hosted tool, Red Herrings connects over LTI 1.3 Advantage with one-click install via Dynamic Registration — or skip all of this and run it offline. Once connected, three things happen on their own.

01

Deep Linking

Add a Red Herrings case from inside your assignment editor and pick practice or a timed exam. No copy-pasting links.

02

Names & Roles

The roster comes straight from the course over NRPS. No CSV to export and import.

03

Grade passback

When a student submits, the score posts directly to your gradebook over Assignment & Grade Services.

Launch and grade through Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle or D2L, or keep it fully offline
