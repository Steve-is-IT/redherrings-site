Two ways to run it

One switch at generation time. The paper worksheet is always included too, so nothing about your offline workflow changes.

Practice

Students answer in the browser and check each question as they go: a quiet "not quite" or "correct", with optional hints. Nothing is locked. Ideal for homework, labs and self-study.

Timed exam

A countdown starts when the student opens their link. Answers are captured and locked on submit, late submissions are flagged, and scores stay yours until you release them. Checking is off, and the answer key is never sent to the browser.

Still copy-resistant

Every link resolves to that student's own seeded variant, and you can shuffle the question order per student. Copying a neighbour's answer gets the wrong result, because it was never the same question.

Trustworthy by construction

The portal keeps the integrity story that makes Red Herrings worth using, with nothing to host for the offline mode.

The key never leaves your computer

The portal is served by Red Herrings on your own machine. A single answer is graded locally and only "right" or "wrong" comes back, so the page a student loads can't be mined for solutions.

No student data leaves

Submissions stay with you. There is no account to create and nothing to upload; a student just opens their link and answers.

A refresh never loses work

Answers are saved in the student's browser as they type, so a reload mid-exam picks up exactly where they left off.

See the whole class at a glance

As students submit, an instructor dashboard shows how the class is doing, all on your machine.

Score distribution

Class average, median and pass rate, and how many students landed in each band.

Hardest questions first

Per-question correct rate, the most common wrong answer, and a flag for any question nobody got, which often means a mis-keyed answer rather than a hard one.

By difficulty

Correct rate across easy, medium and hard, so you can see where the class is really struggling.
