macOS forensics labs, one case at a time

Mac forensics, as the FOR518 objectives frame it, expects an examiner who knows where macOS keeps its own records: download provenance in the quarantine database and Safari history, privacy consent in the TCC database, persistence in LaunchAgents and LaunchDaemons, and the unified log entries that tie them together.

This pathway starts with two First Look cases, one on where an app came from and one on what each app is allowed to see. It then follows a LaunchAgent relaunching a payload, correlates a TCC grant with monitoring-ware, reconstructs a departing employee's exfiltration, and closes with a full spyware compromise across TCC, a beaconing LaunchDaemon and exfil.

The pathway, in order

6 cases, about ~4 h 50 min of lab time. Each step opens the case in the library, where you can read the brief, the evidence list and the question set. Every student gets a different variant of the same case, graded against a key that was verified in the evidence.

    First Look: Where Did This Mac App Come From?

    Trace a download with the quarantine database and Safari history.

Who it is for

  • Candidates preparing for a macOS forensics course or exam who want to work real plists, SQLite databases and log output rather than slides.
  • Instructors adding Apple endpoints to a forensics course who need graded labs that do not require a Mac to solve.
  • Enterprise responders with a Mac fleet who have never had to read a TCC database under pressure.

What you get with every case

  • A different variant per student. Same case, same rubric, different names, hashes, timestamps and answers, so students can talk about method without sharing answers.
  • Evidence in the real on-disk format. Registry hives, EVTX, PCAP, SQLite, JSON audit logs and memory images open in the tools the exam expects.
  • A verified answer key. Every answer is re-derived from the planted evidence before the lab ships, so grading disputes end at the artifact.
  • One-click grading and LMS export. Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, D2L and LTI 1.3 grade passback, or a CSV. See the student portal.
  • Runs on the instructor's machine. Windows installer plus macOS and Linux, fully offline after activation. Nothing to host and no student data leaves the room. Course packs on courses.
