Run scored IR drills on your own evidence formats

A drill plan from First Look triage to a full intrusion, the MITRE ATT&CK techniques it exercises, and how to run a timed, scored exercise. Each analyst gets a different variant of the same incident, so nobody can read answers off a neighbor's screen.

The drill plan

11 cases, about 12 hours in total. Run one a week or one per on-call rotation. 3 run free on download and the trial opens 2 more (Phishing to Foothold and Phishing to Malware to Command and Control), so you can take a phish from the first alert to command and control before you buy.

Free runs on download with no key. Trial opens with the 30-day trial key. Paid plan needs a license; a plan name such as Department means that plan or above.

Running a scored exercise

Set it up

On the Generate screen, pick the Team preset: 8 analysts, exam mode, a 45-minute clock and integrity mode on. Change the headcount and clock to fit your shift. Hand out the portal link; analysts work the evidence in the tools they already use and answer in the browser.

Integrity logging

In exam mode the portal records tab switches, full-screen exits and pastes for each analyst, with times, so a score you report up the chain comes with evidence it was earned under exam conditions.

When the clock stops

A leaderboard ranked by score, then time. The hardest questions sorted by correct rate, with the most common wrong answer, so the debrief starts at the gap. Containment calls are graded too: who isolated the host, who scanned it, who emailed the user.

What you keep

A completion certificate per analyst, and the drill's indicators exported as STIX 2.1 and CSV for your own detection content. Everything runs on your machines; no evidence leaves the network.

The trial opens the phishing-to-C2 cases for 30 days, for up to five analysts. First Look stays free.
