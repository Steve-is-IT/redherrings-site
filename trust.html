Your students' data never leaves your machine

Red Herrings is a desktop app that generates and grades entirely on your own computer. There is no telemetry, no student data leaves the machine, and it runs air-gapped after a one-time activation. The only server contact is licensing — and connected LMS mode is strictly opt-in.

Local and offline by default

The default posture is the safe one. Everything that touches student work happens on the machine in front of you, with no network in the loop.

01

No student data ever leaves the machine

Generation and grading make no network call. Rosters, submissions, evidence and grades stay on your computer. There is no analytics beacon, no cloud sync and no account to create — nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere for it to go.

02

Air-gapped after a one-time activation

A single activation step confirms your license. After that, Red Herrings runs fully offline and air-gapped — and air-gapped machines can even do that one activation by file, with no connection at all.

03

Runs on Windows and Linux

A desktop app you double-click to launch; it opens in your browser and runs locally. No server to stand up, no data to host, nothing to patch on someone else's infrastructure.

The answer key never reaches the student

When students solve in the browser, the portal is served from your own machine. The correct answers stay on your side, and the oracle can't be scripted.

01

Server-side grading, keys held locally

Every answer is graded on your machine and only right-or-wrong comes back. The answer key is never sent to the student's browser, so there is nothing in the page to read, scrape or share.

02

Rate limits and a bounded check oracle

The portal applies per-link and per-client rate limits, and the per-question practice "check" is bounded, so the correct/incorrect oracle can't be scripted into a brute-force guessing loop against an answer.

03

Answers capped and scoped

Submitted answers are length-capped, and submissions are restricted to the lab's own questions. A student can only answer their own lab, and only within sensible bounds.

Licensing & activation, signed and verifiable

Licensing is the one piece that talks to a server, and it is built to be tamper-evident and to keep working when the network isn't there.

01

Ed25519-signed license keys

Every license is cryptographically signed with Ed25519, so the app can verify a key is genuine and unaltered without calling home on every launch.

02

Machine activation with an offline grace

A key activates against a derived machine id. Once activated the app keeps running offline, with a grace period for ordinary network gaps, and deactivation frees the seat to move to a new machine.

Connected mode is opt-in

If you already run a course in an LMS you can connect Red Herrings to it — but that is a choice you make, not a default.

01

LTI 1.3, only when you choose it

Deployed as a hosted tool, Red Herrings connects to Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle or D2L over LTI 1.3, with Deep Linking, roster sync (NRPS) and grade passback (AGS). It is entirely optional.

02

The desktop app stays fully offline otherwise

Skip connected mode and nothing changes about the offline guarantees — the desktop app generates, serves the portal and grades on your machine, with no student data leaving it. See the student portal & LMS page for how connected mode works.
