White-label DFIR training: author and resell your own cases

If you run training, forensics, or managed security services, hands-on DFIR labs are a product your customers want and a product that's expensive to build. There's a way to sell the labs without running the lab factory.

A single realistic case, with planted evidence that holds up and an answer key you can defend, takes a senior specialist days to assemble. Doing that for a catalog, and keeping it fresh, is a business on its own. Generating the cases instead changes that math.

The training product without the build cost

Red Herrings generates cases instead of hand-building them. One authored scenario becomes a different, auto-graded lab for every participant, and every extra seat costs nothing to produce. That changes the economics of a training offering: you aren't paying a specialist to build a new box for each cohort, and you aren't rationing a handful of labs across all your clients.

Author your own cases

The Scenario Editor lets you build cases around the evidence and the threats your customers care about, rather than a generic catalog. Define the artifacts, the questions, the point values and the walkthrough, and the engine handles the per-participant variation and the verified answer key. Your intellectual property is the scenario; the platform does the generation and the grading.

Ship them as packs

Finished scenarios export as a scenario pack, a zip that installs from the Tackle Box in one click. You can bundle a pack as part of a course, sell it as an add-on, or hand it to a client who runs Red Herrings on their own machines.

One authored case becomes a hundred graded labs. That's the difference between selling a lab and running a lab factory.

Your brand, your participants

The Pro license covers full white-labeling, so the platform, the documents and the certificates carry your name rather than ours, with unlimited participants under one license. Clients see your training product, not a tool you resell.

Nothing to host

You generate every participant's case on your own Windows, macOS or Linux machine and hand each one a zip, which they work offline in their own tools. That keeps your delivery costs flat and lets you serve clients who can't send evidence or student work into someone else's cloud.
