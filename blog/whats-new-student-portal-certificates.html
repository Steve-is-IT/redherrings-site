What's new: a student portal, certificates, and 71 cases

Red Herrings has grown a lot since launch. Here's a round-up of what's new for instructors and self-study learners — a self-serve account portal, printable certificates, student and veteran pricing, and a case library that's now 71 strong across digital forensics and incident response.

A self-serve account portal

Buyers now have a home. Sign in at account.redherrings.app with a one-time magic link — no password to set or forget — and see every license on your email, how many seats each has, and which machines they're activated on. Need to move a license to a new laptop? Release a seat yourself. Need an invoice or to change a card? The Manage billing button opens the full billing portal. Nothing to email support for.

Badges and certificates

Finishing a case now means something you can keep. Pass a case and the result screen awards achievement badges — Perfect Score, Clean Sweep, Fast Solver — and a printable Certificate of Completion. Finish a whole certification track and you earn a track certificate; top a class and the instructor can mint 1st, 2nd and 3rd place certificates straight from the leaderboard. Every certificate carries a verification code that anyone can check at redherrings.app/verify. It's proof of the work, generated entirely on your own machine.

Student and veteran pricing

The Personal plan is $69 a year for verified students and U.S. veterans, down from $129. Verification happens in your account and issues a single-use code at checkout — the same self-study library, at a price that fits a student budget.

The library keeps growing

There are now 71 cases spanning digital forensics and incident response, from First Look warm-ups to Expert capstones, each mapped to certification tracks (GCFE, GCFA, GNFA, GCFR, GCIH, CySA+) and — for IR cases — to MITRE ATT&CK. Recent additions deepen the macOS and Linux tracks: a macOS privacy-abuse investigation built on the real TCC consent database, a full spyware-compromise capstone chaining a beaconing LaunchDaemon with data exfiltration, and an expert Linux server-takeover that you reconstruct across five logs by correlating a single attacker IP. Every case still becomes a different, individually verified lab for every student.

And it's all laid out as learning pathways — ordered, case-by-case curricula that take a class from their first triage to a capstone.

Try it with your own class

The 30-day trial needs no card: download the app, generate three sample cases, and run them through grading. When you're ready, the plans start at $129 a year (or $69 for verified students and veterans).
