What the lead sees when the clock stops

Not a pass/fail. A per-analyst, per-question picture of where the team is fast, where it is slow, and which containment calls were wrong.

01

Leaderboard

Score, then time. Ties share a place. Names can be hidden for the debrief.

02

Hardest questions

Sorted by correct rate, with the most common wrong answer, so the debrief starts at the gap.

03

Containment calls

Judgement questions are graded too: who isolated, who scanned, who emailed the user.

04

Certificates and IOC export

A completion certificate per analyst, and the drill’s indicators as STIX 2.1 and CSV for your own detection content.

Why it works for teams, not just classes

The same engine that stops students copying each other stops analysts comparing notes. Every variant has its own hosts, users, times, hashes and indicators, so the drill measures each person.

Runs where your evidence lives

A desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Generation, the analyst portal and grading never make a network call. Air-gapped SOC floors work.

Your tooling, not a simulator

Detection feeds, SIEM exports, proxy and firewall logs, event logs, memory and PCAPs in their real formats. Open them in the tools your team already runs.
