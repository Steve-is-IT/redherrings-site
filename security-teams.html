What the lead sees when the clock stops

This is not a pass/fail result. It is a per-analyst, per-question picture of where the team is fast, where it is slow and which containment calls went wrong.

01

Leaderboard

Score, then time. Ties share a place. Names can be hidden for the debrief.

02

Hardest questions

Sorted by correct rate, with the most common wrong answer, so the debrief starts at the gap.

03

Containment calls

Judgment questions are graded too: who isolated, who scanned, who emailed the user.

04

Certificates and IOC export

A completion certificate per analyst, and the drill’s indicators as STIX 2.1 and CSV for your own detection content.

Why it works for teams, not just classes
