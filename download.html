Verify the download

In PowerShell, in the folder you downloaded to:

(Get-FileHash .\RedHerrings-Setup-1.13.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256).Hash

The output must match the SHA-256 above (letter case doesn't matter). The portable zip's checksum is 6b0f0801ada39fb7607c27c029ec4ee2738fcb2f991101af439efd97c195f8ba.
