Teach a DFIR lab course without building the labs

A term plan you can drop into a syllabus, how grading works, and the import steps for Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle and D2L Brightspace. Every student gets their own evidence and their own answer key, so a classmate's or last term's answer sheet scores about a quarter of the points.

1. Before week one

Install the app and paste your trial key in Settings, Licensing. Import your roster with each student's id set to the identifier your LMS matches on (Canvas SIS Login ID, Blackboard and Moodle username, D2L Username or OrgDefinedId).

2. Each week

Generate the week's case for the roster. Each student gets a zip with their own evidence, plus a link to the student portal where they answer in the browser. You keep the instructor key.

3. After the deadline

Grade the class in one pass, read the per-question analytics, run the anti-collusion report, and export a grade file for your LMS.

The 14-week forensics term

Introduction to Digital Forensics: Undergraduate, second or third year. About 13 lab hours. The cert topic column names the prep track each case sits in (GASF, GCFA, GCFE, GCFR, GNFA). A track is named for a certification, not mapped to it objective by objective. 9 weeks run free and the trial opens 2 more, so you can teach the first stretch of the term before buying anything.

Free runs on download with no key. Trial opens with the 30-day trial key. Paid plan needs a license; a plan name such as Department means that plan or above.

How grading works

One pass for the whole class

Students answer in the portal, or you collect the grading sheet and import it on the Grading screen. Each answer is checked against that student's own key: hashes case-insensitive, timestamps within seconds, free text fuzzy-matched with a review flag. Every score shows why it was awarded.

What you see afterwards

Mean, median and the score distribution, plus the correct rate per question, so you know what to reteach. The anti-collusion report shows how many distinct answers each question had and which student pairs were handed the most matching answers.

Reports and rubrics

Turn on the investigation report template and mark each report against the rubric on the Grading screen. The export can blend the report into the lab score (30% by default) or put it in its own column.

Feedback students read

Turn on feedback at export and each student gets a note listing the questions they missed, for example: "Scored 8.0/10.0 (80.0%). Missed 1: q2 (timestamp)."

Importing grades into your LMS

The app hands you a grade file to upload. It doesn't talk to your LMS over the network, so there's nothing for IT to approve. Choose the column name at export so it matches your gradebook, and raw points or percent.
