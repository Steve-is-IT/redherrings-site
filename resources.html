Free resources for teaching forensics and incident response

Forms, checklists, rubrics and syllabi you can hand to a class tomorrow. All PDFs, no sign-up, free to copy and adapt for classroom use. Most of them pair with something the app does, and all of them work without it.

Forms and checklists

The paperwork that turns a lab exercise into an investigation. Print them, or drop them into your LMS as the assignment attachments.

Form · 1 page

Evidence chain of custody

Case and item fields, an acquisition block with hashes, an eight-row custody log and a final disposition section. A teaching note explains how to use the generated evidence manifest with it.

Download PDF Checklist · 1 page

What a defensible forensic report contains

Eight sections, in the order a reader needs them, with a checkbox for each thing that has to be present. Ends with the ways student reports usually lose marks.

Investigation report rubric

A 100-point rubric with six criteria and four levels, a scoring guide, and a note on how per-student evidence changes the collusion conversation.

Syllabi and course design

Two complete courses with outcomes, grading, tools and a week-by-week plan. Use them as they are or as a starting point for your own.

Introduction to Digital Forensics

Incident Response Fundamentals
